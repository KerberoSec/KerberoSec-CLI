### 💡 Solution:

First, we are given 0x3D (base 16)

> This is also known as "hexadecimal" since we can see the `0x` prefix. 

We are asked to convert `0x3D` to decimal (base 10, or just normal numbers.)

To convert, we can just use a converter to quickly get the solution.

CONVERTER: https://www.rapidtables.com/convert/number/ascii-hex-bin-dec-converter.html

When you click on the link, check mark the "0x/0b prefix" 

then, you copy and paste `0x3D` into the "Hex (bytes)" section. 

It would automatically convert to binary in the "binary (bytes)" section → `61`

Copy the conversion, which is `61` (from binary section).

The hint says to wrap the answer (conversion) using `picoCTF{ text }`.

So the **final flag** is: `picoCTF{61}`! 🎉

> copy and paste (or manually type) the final flag into the submission area to complete the challenge.
