In this room we will be exploring the disciplne of *IMINT/GEOINT*, which is short
for image intelligence and geospatial intelligence. [^1]

Task 2: Your first challenge!
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**What is the name of the street where this image was taken?**

Upon investigating the image file for this task, we spot a storefront sign on the right
and the street sign in the center which reveals the street name *Carnaby Street*. With
this, we can easily construct the flag.

Task 3: Just Google it!
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**Which city is the tube station located in?**

For this question, Google isn't even needed, it's obviously *London*.

**Which tube station do these stairs lead to?**

By searching for the London Underground station on the *Circus Street*, we find out that
the tube station is called *Piccadily Circus*.

**Which year did this station open?**

The Wikipedia entry for the *Piccadily Circus* tube station yields that the station 
opened on March 10th *1906*.

**How many platforms are there in this station?**

Some further reading in the Wikipedia entry from the previous task results in *four*
platforms for the *Piccadily Circus* underground station.

Task 4: Keep at it!
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**Which building is this photo taken in?**

After searching for *YVR Connects* in a search engine of our choice, the *Vancouver

**Which country is this building located in?**

Since *Vancouver* is located in *Canada*, this must be the *YVR* location of the image.

Task 5: Coffe and a light lunch
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**Which city is this coffee shop located in?**

From the banner on top of the store on the other side of the street, we can guess the 
text *Edinburgh Woollen Mill*. Since this retailer has stores all over Scotland, we must 
find the store which matches the architectural characteristics of the one on in the 
picture. After looking at some pictures from *Edinburgh Woollen Mill* stores in a search 
engine of our choice, we discover the store that matches exactly the rounded entry and
red window margins as shown in the target picture in *Blairgowrie*.

**Which street is this coffee shop located in?**

After searching for *Edinburgh Woollen Mill Blairgowrie* in Google Maps, we can locate
the coffee shop on the *Allan Street*.

**What is their phone number?**

The praised coffee shop from across the street is likely *The Wee Coffee Shop* with the 
telephone number `+44 7878 839128` as found on Google Maps.

**What is their email address?**

This task can be solved by visiting the Facebook page that is referenced in the Google 
Maps entry of the coffee shop and is `theweecoffeeshop@aol[.]com`.

**What is the surname of the owners?**

For this task, it is best to look at the holiday break posts on Facebook. Here, coffee 
shop appreciators commented on the well deserved holiday and the owners marked the 
comments with a like or heart. The owner is thus likely called *David Cochrane*.

Task 6: Reverse your thinking
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**Which restaurant was this picture taken at?**

For this task, it is required to reverse search the image from task six, e.g. with Google 
Images [^2]. The first instances found this way already yield that the restaurant is
called *Katz's Deli* and is located in New York.

**What is the name of the Bon Appétit editor that worked 24 hours at this restaurant?**

After searching for the full restaurant name *Katz's Delicatessen* in NYC, followed by 
*Bon Appétit editor*, we find a YouTube video [^3], in which the editor *Andrew Knowlton*
is attempting to work 24 hours there. 

Task 7: Locate this sculpture
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**What is the name of this statue?**

After searching for the image in Google Lens, we find out that the statue is commonly 
referred to as the *Oslo Bike*, and some further search on TripAdvisor [^5] and Flicker
[^4] shows us that the motor moose statue is parked in *Tjuvholmen* in Oslo. After 
searching for the place in Google Maps, it is likely located in the *Astrup Fearnely 
Mueseum of Modern Art* to be more precise.
After looking at some more images provided by the image stock website Alamy, the statue 
is called *Rudolph the Chrome Nosed Reindeer* based on the image's title there.

**Who took this image?**

Based on the hint, it is best to visit the site *visitoslo* [^6] and search for the Oslo
sculptures *Utendørs skulpturer* Maps Finder in Tjuvholmen. Here, the chrome nosed 
reindeer can be found in a picture that resembles the target image very closely. It was
photographed by *Kjersti Stensrud*.

Task 8: And justice for all
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**What is the name of the character that the statue depicts?**

On the right bottom corner of the image, there is the common *The Verge* logo and the
image clearly predicts its usage for various justice related news cases. On the basis of
our common knowledge, this statue depicts the *Lady Justice*.

**Where is this statue located?**

After browsing the Google Lens images, there is a link to a promising site that describes
the sculptures location in *Alexandria Virginia*, on top of the Virginia Eastern District
Court in America.

**What is the name of the building opposite from this statue?**

We rely on the website [^7] from the previous task to zoom in on the built-in map. The 
building opposite from this statue and courthouse is a hotel called *The Westin 
Alexandria Old Town*.

Task 9: The view from my hotel room
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**What is the name of the hotel that my friend stayed in a few years ago?**

Clarke Quay* on the other side of the river.
