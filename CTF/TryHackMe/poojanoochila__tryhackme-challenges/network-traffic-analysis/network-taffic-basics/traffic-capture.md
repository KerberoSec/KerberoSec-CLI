# Traffic Capture Process

## Goal

Capture web traffic associated with a phishing-induced malware download.

---

## Steps

### Step 1

Identify communication path:

Workstation → Firewall → Internet

### Step 2

Place network tap between workstation and firewall.

### Step 3

Capture packets.

### Step 4

Filter HTTP traffic.

### Step 5

Locate malicious response.

### Step 6

Review payload contents.

### Step 7

Document findings.

---

## Result

Successfully captured and analyzed malicious PowerShell download activity.
